Resonance for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2025  ·  Version 2.0

The short version

Resonance is a fully local music player. It stores all data on your device and never sends it to any server controlled by the developer. No accounts, no telemetry, no ads — ever.

What data the app stores

All of the following is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it unless you explicitly enable optional features described below.

  • Music library — song metadata (title, artist, album, genre, year, file path) indexed from your device's MediaStore.
  • Listening history — timestamps and play counts for songs you listen to, used to power the Statistics screen and smart shuffle.
  • Playlists & liked songs — names, song references, and ordering you create in the app.
  • Playback queue — the current song queue, shuffle state, and repeat mode, persisted across restarts.
  • Equalizer settings — band levels, preset choice, and enabled state.
  • App preferences — every setting you configure (appearance, playback, library filters, etc.).

You can export, delete, or clear all of this data at any time from within the app.

Permissions the app requests

  • Read media / audio files — required to read music files from your device storage.
  • Post notifications — to show the now-playing notification and media controls.
  • Internet — only used for optional features you explicitly enable (see below). Never used for telemetry.
  • Foreground service / Bluetooth — to keep playback running and respond to headphone controls.

Optional network features

These features make network requests only when you choose to enable them. Each one is opt-in and can be disabled at any time in Settings.

🎵
Last.fm Scrobbling
When enabled, the title, artist, and album of songs you play are sent to Last.fm under your account. Governed by the Last.fm Privacy Policy.
opt-in
🖥️
Navidrome / Subsonic
When enabled, the app connects to a Navidrome server you provide and control. No data is sent to any third-party server; traffic goes only to your own server.
opt-in
🎨
Artist images
When enabled, artist names are sent to the Deezer public API to fetch artist photos. No personal information is transmitted — only the artist name string.
opt-in
🔤
Lyrics
When lyrics are fetched automatically, song title and artist are sent to the lrclib.net public API. No account or personal information is involved.
opt-in

What we never do

The developer does not collect analytics, crash reports, usage data, device identifiers, or any personal information. There are no ads, no third-party SDKs for tracking, and no remote configuration.

Data you share with others

Resonance Share lets you send a song file directly to a nearby device using Nearby Connections or a QR Code over your local network. The song file is sent peer-to-peer; no data passes through any server controlled by the developer.

Backup & restore

The Backup & Restore feature exports your liked songs and playlists to a JSON file saved to a location you choose on your device. This file contains song file paths and playlist names — nothing sensitive. It never leaves your device automatically.

Children's privacy

Resonance is a general-purpose music player and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. It collects no personal information at all, making it safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
